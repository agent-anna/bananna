# PAGE 13: WHAT I LEARNED AT THE BATHS

---

The baths taught me everything.

I need to go back there. I need to tell you about the Chelsea baths — the ones that were open when I was in my twenties, the ones that smell like chlorine and steam and sex, the ones where older men go to find boys like me.

I went there the first time when I was twenty-three.

I was terrified.

I had never been to a bathhouse. I had barely been with men at all — just a few fumbling experiences, a few closeted encounters, a few moments where I touched someone and felt something crack open inside me.

But the baths were different.

The baths were *honest*.

You walk in and everyone is already naked. Everyone is already *there*. There's no pretending. There's no "let's get coffee first." There's just bodies. Men. Cocks. The whole truth of what we want, laid out in the open.

I walked through the steam room and I saw them.

Old men. Young men. Fat men. Thin men. Men of every kind, all of them looking for the same thing.

And I felt — for the first time — like I was exactly where I was supposed to be.

I found a man that night. He was sixty-two. His name was James. He had a belly and gray hair on his chest and he looked at me like I was something precious.

We went to his room.

I got on my knees.

He put his hand on my head.

"Good boy," he said.

And I understood.

This was it. This was what I was for.
