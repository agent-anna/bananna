# PAGE 22: THE OLDER MAN AS TEACHER

---

Every older man I've been with has taught me something.

This is not an exaggeration. I mean *every* one. Even the ones who weren't good at it. Even the ones who didn't know what they were doing. Even the ones who just took and didn't give.

They all taught me something.

**Herb taught me patience.**

Herb taught me that worship takes time. That a man isn't a goal to be reached but a treasure to be savored. That the best things in life are the things you take slow.

**Raymond taught me trust.**

Raymond taught me that surrender isn't weakness. That letting someone all the way in — letting them see your most vulnerable self — is the bravest thing you can do.

**James taught me presence.**

James taught me that the moment is all there is. That past and future disappear when you're on your knees in front of a man who sees you. That *now* is the only thing that's real.

**Martin taught me healing.**

Martin taught me that sex can be medicine. That being wanted can fix things that talk therapy never will. That a soft boy's mouth can heal wounds a man has been carrying for decades.

These are my teachers.

These are my masters.

These are the men who made me who I am.

And for that — for all of it — I am grateful.
