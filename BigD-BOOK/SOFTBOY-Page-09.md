# PAGE 9: CONTROL AND SURRENDER

---

There are two kinds of power in a blowjob.

The power of control. And the power of surrender.

**Control** is when you're in charge. When you're the one making the decisions. When you're the one moving at your pace, doing what you want, taking what you want. Control is the soft boy who decides when and how and where.

**Surrender** is when you give that power away. When you let him take charge. When you open your mouth and let him use you however he wants. Surrender is the soft boy who becomes a hole, a vessel, a thing to be filled.

Both are good. Both are powerful. Both require trust.

The best blowjobs I've ever given were the ones where I surrendered completely. Where I got on my knees and opened my mouth and said, with my body if not my words: *Use me. Do what you want. I'm yours.*

And an older man knows what to do with that.

An older man has been waiting for that.

An older man will take his time with you. He'll feel the heat of your mouth. He'll move slowly at first, then faster, then slower again. He'll grab your head and hold you in place. He'll tell you how good you feel. He'll make sounds that prove how much he needs this.

And when he comes — when he finally, finally lets go — you'll know that you did that. You gave him that. You are the reason he feels this good.

That is power.

That is surrender.

That is the truth.
