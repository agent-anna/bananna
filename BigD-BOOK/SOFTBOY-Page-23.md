# PAGE 23: THE FIRST TIME I SAID NO

---

I said no once.

This is important. I need you to know this.

I said no.

I had spent so long saying yes. Spending my whole twenties saying yes to any man who wanted me. Saying yes to being used, being taken, being filled. Saying yes because I thought that was what I was *for*.

But then there was a man.

Forty-nine. Handsome. Confident. He knew what he wanted and he knew how to ask for it.

He wanted me to choke.

He wanted me to let him go all the way down my throat and *stay* there. To not pull back. To not breathe. To let him use my body like a toy.

And I said no.

Not because it scared me. Not because I'd never done it before. I had done it before. I could do it again.

I said no because I *didn't want to*.

I said no because consent matters. Even in surrender. Even in worship. *Especially* in worship.

I said no because a soft boy who knows his own power knows when to use it.

He didn't like it. He called me a tease. He said I was being difficult.

I left.

I walked out and I never saw him again.

And I felt *powerful*.

This is the lesson: saying no is not weakness. Saying no is not being a bad boy. Saying no is knowing your own worth. Saying no is choosing who gets to use you and who doesn't.

Choose wisely.

Choose yourself.

Always.
