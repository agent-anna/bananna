# PAGE 19: THE FEELING

---

What does it feel like?

People ask me this all the time. How does it feel to be filled? How does it feel to have a man inside you?

I don't know how to answer.

Because it's not just physical. It's not just "it feels good" or "it feels intense." It's something more than that. Something deeper. Something that changes you.

**Physically**, it feels like being opened. Being stretched. Being made room for. Your body adapts. Your muscles release. You make space for him inside you.

**Sexually**, it feels like nothing else. Every movement sends shockwaves through you. Every thrust hits something that makes you see stars. You feel *him* — his weight, his heat, his *hunger* — moving in and out of you.

**Emotionally**, it feels like surrender. Like letting go. Like trusting someone completely with the most vulnerable part of yourself and knowing — *knowing* — that they won't hurt you.

**Spiritually** — and I know this sounds dramatic but I mean it — it feels like communion. Like two people becoming one thing. Like you're giving him something that no one else can give, and he's giving you something that no one else can give, and in that exchange, you're both made whole.

This is what I live for.

This is what I need.

This is the feeling.
