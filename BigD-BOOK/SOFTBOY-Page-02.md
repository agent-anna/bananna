# PAGE 2: SINCE I WAS SIXTEEN

---

Since I was sixteen, I have wanted one thing.

Older men.

Not boys my own age. Not men my own age. Men who had been alive long enough to know what they wanted. Men who had paid their dues. Men who had done the work. Men who had earned the gray in their hair and the softness in their bodies and the wisdom in their eyes.

I wanted to be *handled* by men who knew how to handle me.

This is the confession. This is the beginning. This is the page where I tell you exactly what I am and why.

I am a soft boy who likes older men.

I have been a soft boy who likes older men since I was sixteen years old and I saw my first older man and thought: *I want to know what that feels like.*

I'm forty-seven now.

I know what it feels like.

I'm going to tell you everything.
