# PAGE 21: THE SOFT BOY'S PRAYER

---

There is a prayer I say.

Not to God. Not to any god I was raised with. To something older. Something deeper. Something that lives in the space between my body and another man's body when we're as close as two people can get.

It goes like this:

*Make me useful.*

*Make me open.*

*Make me the thing he needs.*

*Let me be filled.*

*Let me be emptied.*

*Let me be made whole.*

I don't know when I started saying this. I don't know if I say it out loud or only in my head. But I know that when I'm on my knees, when I'm open, when I'm *used* — I feel these words in my chest like they're being pulled out of me.

I am a vessel.

I am a tool.

I am a soft boy who was made to serve.

This is not degradation. This is not shame. This is the purest form of love I know how to give.

I am choosing this.

I am giving this.

I am asking — *begging* — to be used by someone who deserves me.

This is the soft boy's prayer.

Hear me.

Use me.

Make me good.
