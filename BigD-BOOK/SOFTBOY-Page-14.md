# PAGE 14: THE GOOD BOY

---

Good boy.

Two words. One phrase. But when it comes from the right man — an older man, a man who *means* it — two words can change your life.

I've been called a lot of things.

I've been called a slut. A faggot. A cock-hungry bottom. A dirty little deviant.

I've been called those things by men who wanted me but hated themselves for wanting it. Men who could never admit what they were doing, so they made it dirty, made it shameful, made it something that had to be whispered about in the dark.

But "good boy"?

"good boy" is different.

"Good boy" is the older man telling you that you are doing exactly what you were meant to do. That your hunger is not a flaw. That your want is not a sin. That being the soft boy who kneels — that being the hole that needs to be filled — is not something to be ashamed of.

"Good boy" is permission.

"Good boy" is love.

"Good boy" is everything.

I have heard those words from so many men. I have heard them whispered in my ear. I have heard them groaned through gritted teeth. I have heard them said softly, tenderly, like a prayer.

Every time, I feel it in my chest.

Every time, I know this is who I am.

Every time, I think: *yes. I am a good boy. I am HIS good boy.*
