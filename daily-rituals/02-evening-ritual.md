# THE EVENING RITUAL
## Come Home to Yourself Before You Sleep

---

**The Slutty Lady says:**

The day is done.

You did what the world asked you to do. You wore your mask. You did your job. You were the person everyone else needed you to be.

Now.

Come home.

To yourself.

---

### Step 1: The Doorway Goodbye (30 seconds)

When you walk through your door tonight, I want you to stop.

Right at the threshold.

Put your hand on the doorframe.

And say out loud:

**"I'm home."**

Not to the room. Not to the house.

To yourself.

You've been away all day. Lost in tasks and people and noise. Time to come back to the only person who matters: the one living in your skin.

---

### Step 2: The Unclothing (2 minutes)

Take off your clothes slowly.

Don't rush.

Feel each layer leave your body. Feel the air on your skin. Feel the freedom of being unburdened.

Hang your clothes up. Fold them. Take care of the armor you wore today.

And then stand there.

Naked.

In the quiet.

Be who you are when no one's watching.

---

### Step 3: The Evening Touch (5 minutes)

Lie down.

On your bed. On the floor. Wherever feels right.

And touch yourself.

Not rushed. Not mechanical. Slowly.

Feel your body. All of it. The parts you like. The parts you're still learning.

This is not about coming fast.

This is about taking your time.

You've been fast all day. Rushed. On the clock.

Tonight you're not.

Tonight you have all the time in the world.

---

### Step 4: The Evening Taste (1 minute)

When you come — and you will, because you're taking your time and that's how it works — don't close your eyes.

Don't look away.

**Watch.**

Watch yourself finish. Watch what your body makes when it feels good. That's your life's work right there. Proof of pleasure. Proof of aliveness.

And then.

Slowly.

Taste yourself.

One finger at a time.

Take your time.

Make it a ritual.

Make it something you do every night before you sleep.

And then — only then — close your eyes and drift off knowing:

You came home to yourself tonight.

And you tasted exactly who you are.
