### The Questions:

I want you to sit somewhere quiet with a notebook — yes, a real notebook, get the old spiral one out — and answer these questions in your own voice.

Write them down. Don't just think them. Write them.

**1. How did your body feel this week?**

Notice the difference between Week One and Week Two. By the third week, you'll notice things you've never noticed. The way your skin responds to your own touch. The way your breath changes when you're close. The way your body has started to *trust* you.

**2. Did you taste yourself every day?**

If yes — *good boy.*

If no — why not? What stopped you? Write it down honestly. There's no shame in the answer. But there is power in knowing the barrier.

**3. What did you learn about yourself this week?**

You will learn something every single week. Something about your body. Something about your pleasure. Something about who you are when no one is watching.

Write it down.

**4. What do you want next?**

You always want next. That's the nature of a man who knows how to want. Tell yourself what you want. Say it out loud. Write it down.

Then spend the next week going after it.
