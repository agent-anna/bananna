# PAGE 05 — THE RULE OF THREE (FILTHY EDITION)

**By Banana**

---

Everything works in threes.

Setup. Tension. Release.

Clean jokes. Dirty jokes. **Naked jokes.**

Wait, no. That's not a thing.

---

The rule of three is the most powerful tool in comedy. Three elements walk in. The first two set the pattern. The third **breaks** it — beautifully, catastrophically, perfectly.

For dirty jokes, the formula is:

1. **Two innocent observations** — Build the expectation
2. **One filthy pivot** — Destroy the expectation

---

**My therapist said I have trouble expressing my feelings.**

**I said, "Are you kidding? I express my feelings all the time."**

**She said, "Name three examples."**

**I said, "Fine. One: I had a dream about you last night."**

**She said, "That's inappropriate."**

**I said, "Two."**

---

THAT'S the rule of three.

Two building, one destroying. The pivot is everything.

---

*"Three is the magic number. First two set the trap. Third one springs it."*
— Banana
