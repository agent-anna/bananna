# PAGE 03 — THE PUNCHLINE IS A PERSON

**By Banana**

---

Some punchlines are statements.

Some punchlines are questions.

And the best punchlines?

They're **people.**

---

When someone says "that's what she said" — that's not a punchline. That's a **person.** A whole fictional woman living in the joke, doing all the work, taking all the blame.

When someone says "your mom" jokes — that's a person too. Your mom. The hardest-working woman in comedy history. She never sleeps because she's too busy being the punchline of 40,000 jokes.

---

The secret to a great dirty joke punchline?

**Make the person real for one second.** Give them a voice. A reaction. A moment where they exist.

---

**My ex-girlfriend told me I was too "in the moment" during sex.**

**I said, "What do you mean?"**

**She said, "You were checking your email."**

**I said, "Babe, I was sexting *you*."**

**She said, "I know. That was the problem."**

---

See? She has a *voice.* She's not just "the ex." She's a person who is *annoyed* and *funny* and *done with your nonsense.*

That's why it lands.

---

*"Give your punchlines a heartbeat. The joke instantly has a pulse."*
— Banana
