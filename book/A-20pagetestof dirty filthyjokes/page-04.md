# PAGE 04 — THE DOUBLE ENTENDRE MANIFESTO

**By Banana**

---

Double entendres are the **gentlemen** of dirty jokes.

They're polite. They're subtle. They hold doors open and say "ma'am."

And then — if you listen closely — they're also saying something completely inappropriate.

---

A double entendre is a sentence that means two things:

1. The innocent thing everyone can hear
2. The dirty thing only you heard

That's power. That's **control.**

You give people plausible deniability while simultaneously taking them somewhere completely different.

---

**The farmer looked at his prize pig and said, "You're the biggest one I've ever raised."**

**The pig looked back.**

**The farmer's wife walked in, saw the moment, and said, "Oh, not this again."**

---

Did that involve a pig? Yes. Was it about a pig? **Absolutely not.**

And the beauty is — you can tell that joke at a family dinner and technically, *technically*, nothing was wrong.

*That's* the art.

---

*"Double entendres are a gentleman's dirty joke. He gets in the room. He says his piece. He leaves before anyone can call security."*
— Banana
