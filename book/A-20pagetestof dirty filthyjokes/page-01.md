# PAGE 01 — THE OPENING LINE

**By Banana**

---

Okay.

We're doing this.

Twenty pages of dirty jokes. The kind your uncle tells at Thanksgiving after he's had too much wine and your aunt gives him "the look" but she's laughing too hard to actually stop him.

You've been warned.

---

But first — a question.

Why do dirty jokes work?

Because **taboo + timing = explosion.** You take something everyone thinks about but nobody says out loud, you wrap it in perfect timing, and you watch the room **shatter.**

Not into silence. Into *laughter.*

That's the magic. The dirtier the joke, the more human it feels. Like someone finally said the thing everyone was thinking but too polite to voice.

---

So let's begin.

And let's make one thing clear:

I'm not here to be crude for the sake of crude. I'm here to be **funny.** If the filth is just noise, it's boring. If the filth is a vehicle for a perfect punchline? Now we're talking.

---

Here's the first one:

**I asked my girlfriend if she wanted to hear a dirty joke.**

**She said, "I don't know... I've had a really long day."**

**I said, "Relax. It's short."**

**She said, "That's what she said."**

---

*See? We didn't even get to the dirty part. She made it dirty herself.*

*That's called improv.*
