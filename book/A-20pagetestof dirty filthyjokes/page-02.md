# PAGE 02 — THE SETUP ARTIST

**By Banana**

---

Here's what separates amateur dirty jokes from **professional** dirty jokes.

Amateurs go straight for the punchline. They lead with the dirt like it's a weapon.

Pros? Pros build the **room** first.

---

You ever been at a dinner party where someone leans in and says, "okay, I've got a good one" and then proceeds to tell a joke so clean it could air on network television? 

You nod. You smile politely. You don't laugh.

But when someone catches you off guard — when they seem like they're telling a normal story and then the punchline comes out of **nowhere** and it's something so filthy your eyes go wide?

*That's* the craft.

---

**Three guys walk into a bar.**

The first one orders a beer.

The second one orders a whiskey.

The third one pulls down his pants, sits on the bar, and says, "I'll have whatever she's having."*

*— There is no "she." He just wanted to sit down.*

---

See what I did there?

The setup was **aggressively normal.** Three guys. Bar. Beer. Whiskey. Classic setup.

You were already halfway to the bar joke you heard a thousand times.

And then I went sideways.

*That's* how you build a dirty joke. You make them think they know where it's going. Then you show them the **real** destination.

---

*"The best dirty joke is the one where they think they're safe... right up until they're not."*
— Banana
