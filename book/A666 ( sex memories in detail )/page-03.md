# PAGE 03 — THE FIRST TOUCH

**By Banana**

---

Here's what they don't show in movies.

The awkwardness.

The accidental elbow. The "sorry" when there's nothing to be sorry for. The moment where you're both figuring out the **geometry** of two bodies in a space meant for one.

---

**Memory #3:**
*Filed under: "Geometry is not romantic."*

---

I stood up. Finally. Took a breath. Reached for her waist like I'd seen in a hundred films.

And missed.

Not "missed the moment." I literally **missed** — my hand went somewhere around her hip, found fabric, redirected, and somehow ended up on her shoulder like I was trying to **high-five her collarbone.**

---

She didn't laugh.

She just gently moved my hand to where it was supposed to be.

"Here," she said. Not unkind. Not teasing. Just... teaching.

---

*"Every first touch feels clumsy. That's how you know it mattered."*
— Banana
