# PAGE 05 — THE CLOTHES CONVERSATION

**By Banana**

---

Clothes are a negotiation you have with gravity.

You can't fight physics. You can only delay it.

---

**Memory #5:**
*Filed under: "The hallway was colder than the room."*

---

We migrated from the couch to somewhere else. I don't remember the path. I remember the **hallway** — narrow, slightly darker, and she stopped walking and turned around and looked at me like we were about to have a business meeting.

---

She said: "Are you sure?"

Not "do you want to." Not "let's." **"Are you sure."**

Two words that contain entire universes.

---

I said yes.

She said: "Okay."

And then she reached behind her back and **unzipped.**

---

*"The moment of no return is usually preceded by two words. Listen to them."*
— Banana
