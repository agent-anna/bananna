# PAGE 02 — THE ART OF WAITING

**By Banana**

---

The moment before is its own universe.

Time slows down. Sounds become sharper. The ambient noise of life becomes **very quiet** and the only thing you hear is your own heartbeat, which is suddenly very loud and you're wondering if she can hear it too.

---

**Memory #2:**
*Filed under: "The pause that lasted forever."*

---

She put down the wine glass.

She walked toward me.

And instead of doing something — **anything** — I just... stayed on the couch. Frozen. Like a guy who practices what he'd say in moments like this and then when the moment arrives, his mouth forgets every word.

---

She stopped in front of me.

Looked down.

Smiled.

And said: "You're still on the couch."

---

I said, "You told me to stay."

She said, "That was five minutes ago."

---

*"The best moments in memory are the ones where you didn't know what to do. That's when you learned."*
— Banana
