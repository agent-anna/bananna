# PAGE 04 — THE SOFTEST PART

**By Banana**

---

The softest parts.

Not where you think they are.

The back of the neck. The inside of the wrist. The spot just below the ear where her pulse meets the skin.

---

**Memory #4:**
*Filed under: "Skin remembers things the mind forgets."*

---

I kissed her neck and she made a sound.

Not pornographic. Not performative. Just... a small, involuntary exhale that said "yes" before language could catch up.

That sound.

I think about that sound more than I should. More than is reasonable. At weird moments — grocery store, traffic light, 3 AM staring at the ceiling.

The sound she didn't know she was going to make.
