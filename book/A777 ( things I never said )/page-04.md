# PAGE 04 — "I SAID YES BECAUSE I WAS SCARED"

**By Banana**

---

Every "yes" isn't enthusiasm.

Sometimes it's the sound someone makes when they're cornered and they can't find the exit fast enough, so they agree to something they don't want, in a place they don't want to be, with a person who makes them feel small.

And then the night ends and the "yes" echoes in their head like a mistake on loop.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I said yes because I was scared."*

To the job interview that led to three years of misery. To the "let's just be friends" that I knew meant "I don't want you but I don't want to lose you either." To the "of course I'll come to your party" when I had already made plans I'd actually wanted to keep.

Every yes was a tiny surrender.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because "scared" sounds weak. Sounds like you don't know how to advocate for yourself. Sounds like you're the kind of person who lets things happen to them.

And I didn't want to be that person.

So I said yes, and then I lived with it, and then I blamed everyone else for the consequences of choices I made.

---

*"The most expensive word in your vocabulary is 'yes.' The second most expensive is 'fine.' Learn when they're lies."*
— Banana
