# PAGE 05 — "I'M PROUD OF YOU AND I'VE ALWAYS BEEN JEALOUS"

**By Banana**

---

You can't say both.

"I'm so proud of you" and "I'm so jealous of you" cancel each other out. They create a static in the sentence that makes both sentiments disappear.

So you just say the proud one. Because that's what you're supposed to say. That's what good friends do.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I'm proud of you and I've always been jealous."*

Two truths that exist in the same breath, that live in the same chest, that sometimes hit you at the same moment — watching your friend get the thing you wanted, the promotion, the relationship, the life that looks effortless in a way yours never does.

And you ARE proud. Genuinely. You love watching them win.

But you're also sitting there wondering why your life doesn't look like that. Why the universe gave them the map and left you with a blank piece of paper.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because jealousy between friends is supposed to be something you grow out of. Like acne. Like bad poetry.

You grow up. You mature. You stop comparing.

Except you don't.

You just get better at hiding it.

---

*"The friendships that survive are the ones where both people admit they were sometimes afraid the other one would win."*
— Banana
