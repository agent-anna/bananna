# PAGE 03 — "I LOVED YOU MORE THAN YOU KNEW"

**By Banana**

---

The thing about unrequited love is it doesn't feel unrequited.

Not at the time.

Every text they send feels like a signal. Every laugh sounds like an answer. Every "I really needed to talk to you tonight" sounds like "I really needed you."

Then years pass and you look back and realize: it was never a signal. It was just... kindness.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I loved you more than you knew."*

Not "I love you" — past tense. Because I don't anymore. Not like that. But I did. And you never knew. Or maybe you did. Maybe you knew and chose not to see it because seeing it would have meant doing something about it, and you weren't ready.

Neither was I.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because saying it would have made it real. And real things can be rejected.

If I never said it, it could stay in the space between us. A maybe. A possibility. A thing that *could have been* if the timing was different, if the world was different, if I was different.

Once you say it out loud, it becomes a thing that *was.* And that's smaller. Smaller than a maybe.

---

*"The safest place for a love that can't exist is in the place you never say out loud."*
— Banana
