# PAGE 10 — "I COULDN'T SAVE YOU AND IT BROKE ME"

**By Banana**

---

This is the hardest one.

Not the hardest to say. The hardest to admit to myself.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I couldn't save you and it broke me."*

Not a romantic thing. Not about a partner.

My friend. The one who was drowning. Who called me at 2 AM and I talked them down. Who I thought I had saved. Who died three months later anyway.

I thought I bought them time. I didn't buy them a life.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because if I admit it broke me, I have to feel it.

All of it. The guilt. The "what if I'd done more." The fact that I couldn't. The fact that no one could. The fact that some people are in pain too deep to reach and we do everything right and it still isn't enough.

Some people drown even when you're standing right there.

---

**Here's what I know now:**

I couldn't save them. But I showed up. I tried. I sat on the phone at 2 AM and I stayed as long as they needed and I told them they mattered.

And it wasn't enough.

But I didn't know it wouldn't be enough when I did it.

I just knew they needed someone.

And I was there.

---

*"You don't get to save everyone you're brave enough to love. You just get to be there when they need you. That's all. That's the whole thing."*
— Banana
