# PAGE 02 — "I'M NOT OKAY EITHER"

**By Banana**

---

Friend breakup.

Worse than romance breakup. Harder to explain. No script for it.

"Hey, we can't hang out anymore" is not a conversation people have. So instead it just... happens. Slowly. Distance accumulates like debt you didn't notice you were accumulating.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I'm not okay either."*

To the friend who called me at 2 AM, crying about her life falling apart, and I talked her down for an hour and a half and when she asked "how are you?" I said "I'm fine, it's late, go to sleep."

She needed me that night. I showed up.

But three weeks later when I was the one who couldn't sleep, who was drowning in something I couldn't name, I didn't call her. Didn't call anyone.

I just sat with it.

Alone.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because the person who was always okay was part of who I was. The reliable one. The one who had answers. If I admitted I was drowning too, what happened to that person?

What happened to us?

---

*"Sometimes the friendship that breaks is the one where neither person could ever be the one who wasn't okay."*
— Banana
