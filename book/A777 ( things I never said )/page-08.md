# PAGE 08 — "I STAYED FOR THE WRONG REASONS"

**By Banana**

---

Everyone leaves eventually.

Not a nihilist observation. A logistical one. Every relationship you have — friend, lover, family — has a half-life. Some last decades. Some last a single bad Tuesday. All of them end.

The question isn't "will this end?" It's "why are you still here?"

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I stayed for the wrong reasons."*

To every place I should have left. Every job that was killing me slowly. Every room where I felt smaller every day. Every person who loved me in a way that felt like a cage with the door open.

I knew. I always knew.

I just didn't leave.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because admitting you stayed for the wrong reasons means admitting you knew. That you had the information. That you could have chosen differently at any point and you didn't.

That's on you.

Not on the job. Not on the room. Not on them.

You.

---

*"The prison with the door open is still a prison. Staying is still a choice."*
— Banana
