# PAGE 01 — "YOU LOOK BETTER THAN I REMEMBER"

**By Banana**

---

The thing about exes is they never look the way you remember them.

You remember the version that was yours. The one who laughed at your jokes, who got annoyed when you did that thing you always did, who knew exactly how to make your coffee and exactly how to ruin your morning.

Then you see them at a party three years later and they're... **different.** Sharper. Softer. Something in their eyes that's older than it used to be.

And you think: *when did you get better?*

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"You look better than I remember."*

Because that would mean admitting I remembered. Admitting I thought about it. Admitting that somewhere between the argument and the silence and the three years of not speaking, I had occasionally — not often, but occasionally — wondered what you looked like now.

That's not something you say to an ex.

Even when it's true.

---

**But here's what I know now:**

The version of them you remember isn't the real them. It's the version your brain kept. The highlight reel. The best takes.

The version standing across the room? That's the real movie.

And the real movie is always more interesting.

---

*"You look better than I remember" is the most honest thing you can say to anyone you've ever lost."*
— Banana
