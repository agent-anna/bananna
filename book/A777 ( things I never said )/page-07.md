# PAGE 07 — "I DON'T TRUST YOU AND I DON'T KNOW WHY"

**By Banana**

---

Trust doesn't come back.

Not the same trust. You can rebuild something. You can build something new. But the original trust — the version that existed before you knew they could hurt you — that's gone.

Like a vase. You can glue it back together. Everyone will tell you it's fine. And technically it is.

But you know where the cracks are.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I don't trust you and I don't know why."*

After they swore they'd changed. After they did everything right. After they showed up, again and again, proving they were different now, better now, and I sat there thinking *I believe you but I don't believe you.*

Trust is not a choice. It's a feeling. And feelings don't get备忘录 updated by promises.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because "I don't trust you" sounds like an accusation. Like you're saying they're still the person who hurt you. Like you're not letting them grow.

But the truth is: I wasn't talking about them anymore.

I was talking about me. About the part of me that learned the pattern and can't unlearn it. About the version of myself that formed in response to being hurt and refuses to dissolve, even when the threat is gone.

---

*"Sometimes 'I don't trust you' means 'I'm afraid of what I became when you weren't safe.'"*
— Banana
