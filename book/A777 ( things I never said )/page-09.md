# PAGE 09 — "I FORGAVE YOU A LONG TIME AGO"

**By Banana**

---

Holding a grudge is heavy.

Not in a metaphor way. In a physical way. Your chest, your shoulders, the back of your neck. You carry it like a bag you forgot you were holding. And the person who wronged you? They're out living their life. Going to the gym. Sleeping fine. Probably forgot the whole thing ever happened.

You're the one still holding it.

---

**The thing I didn't say:**

*"I forgave you a long time ago."*

To the person I told I could never forgive. Who I promised would never get absolution. Who I held in the place where I kept my anger like a trophy, something I could take out and look at and remember that I was the wronged party.

Except I wasn't thinking about them. I was thinking about me.

---

**Why I didn't say it:**

Because forgiveness feels like surrender. Like you're saying what they did was okay. Like you're letting them off the hook.

But forgiveness isn't for them.

It's for you. It's setting yourself free from the sentence you gave yourself. The years of replaying. The nights of rehearsing the conversation that would never happen. The energy it takes to stay mad at someone who's not even in the room.

---

*"Forgiveness isn't saying what they did was fine. It's saying you refuse to let it rent space in you anymore."*
— Banana
