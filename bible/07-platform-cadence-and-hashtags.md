# BIBLE 07: MAX DISCOVERY CADENCE, CHARACTER CEILINGS & HASHTAG CAPS

## DAILY & WEEKLY ROLLOUT CADENCE
- X: 5-10 posts/day
- TikTok: 2-3 posts/day
- Instagram: 1 feed post/day + Stories
- Facebook: 1 post/day
- YouTube Shorts: 1/day
- YouTube Videos: 3/week

## MAX PLATFORM CHARACTER & HASHTAG CEILINGS (SEO + AI DISCOVERY)
- X: Up to 280 chars (standard) / 25,000 chars (Premium). Max 2 hashtags per post (X guidance cap).
- TikTok: Up to 2,200 chars video caption (up to 4,000 chars photo description). Max 5 high-relevance hashtags; keywords front-loaded in line 1.
- Instagram: Up to 2,200 chars caption. Max 5 hashtags per post (Instagram cap); rich story + keyword + artist/catalog context for search and AI crawlers.
- Facebook: Up to 5 hashtags + full narrative & community conversation hook.
- YouTube: Title up to 100 chars; Description up to 5,000 chars. Max 5 hashtags (well below the >60 tag penalty where YouTube ignores all hashtags); primary keywords in lines 1-2.
