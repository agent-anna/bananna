# BIBLE 05: MISSION MANIFESTO — MARTIN STRAYD 365-DAY DAILY SINGLE LAUNCH

## 1. Strategic Mission
Execute a 365-day single-a-day global release and media campaign for **Martin Strayd**, unlocking over 10 albums' worth of material (100+ licensed songs) across international radio, global streaming platforms, and direct listener ownership.

## 2. Official Press Release Artist Biography (Include in Full Press Releases Only)
- **Headline Pedigree:** Australian lyricist, songwriter, composer, and independent recording artist **Martin Strayd** channels 55 years of life experience and four decades of songwriting into an unprecedented daily single rollout.
- **Hollywood & Rock Legacy:** Before launching his solo catalog, Martin Strayd worked extensively behind the music industry scenes as a 1990s celebrity Hollywood guitar technician for rock legends including **Steve Jones (Sex Pistols)** and **Doug Fieger (The Knack)**, and is featured on **ABC Triple J Unearthed**.
- **Catalog Scale:** Over 10 albums' worth of original, licensed singles and cinematic soundtracks streaming worldwide across Spotify, Apple Music / iTunes, YouTube Music, Shazam, and international broadcast radio.
- **International Radio Footprint (Countries):** Heard across radio stations in **Australia, the Philippines, Ecuador, the United States, Chile, Canada, Indonesia, France, and Thailand** (and expanding).
- **Direct Listener Ownership:** Buyers receive MP3 + cover art bundles they own outright and may share freely with friends for non-commercial enjoyment.

## 3. Thin OS + Modular Apps Architecture
- **Bible (`bible/`):** Single source of truth for mission, official press bio, host persona, platform limits/hooks, links, and ledger schemas. Apps fetch only the chapters they require.
- **Thin Core / Dashboard:** Fast navigation across tools without bloating individual HTML pages.
- **App #1 (`strayd/studio.html`):** Builds a full, 3/4-page wire-grade Press Release (top, dated, with full artist bio and radio countries) and platform-optimized social posts (below, undated, zero bio bloat) first, then uses those posts as spoken radio filler (`Speak`) second.
- **Visual Standard:** Realistic, cinematic aesthetics only. Zero cartoon styling.
