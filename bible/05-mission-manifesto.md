# BIBLE 05: MISSION MANIFESTO — MARTIN STRAYD 365-DAY DAILY SINGLE LAUNCH

## 1. Strategic Mission
Execute a disciplined, 365-day single-a-day global release and media rollout for **Martin Strayd**, presenting four decades of songwriting and a 55-year music journey across radio, streaming platforms, and direct listener ownership.

## 2. Artist Pedigree & Positioning
- **Artist:** Martin Strayd — Australian lyricist, songwriter, composer, and veteran musician with over 40 years of songwriting and 55 years of music/industry experience, including behind-the-scenes work as a Hollywood guitar technician alongside rock figures such as Steve Jones (Sex Pistols) and Doug Fieger (The Knack).
- **Catalog & Distribution:** Licensed singles and soundtracks across major streaming services and radio alongside direct MP3 + cover art bundles (`strayd/songs-db.json` and `strayd/core-catalog.json`).
- **Standard of Execution:** Publishing-wire and broadcast-executive polish—authoritative, clear, and zero fluff. Every daily release is treated as a real catalog event.
- **Visual Standard:** Realistic, cinematic aesthetics only. Zero cartoon styling.

## 3. Thin OS + Modular Apps Architecture
- **Bible (`bible/`):** Single source of truth for mission, host persona, platform limits/hooks, links, and ledger schemas. Apps fetch only the specific chapters they require.
- **Thin Core / Dashboard:** Fast navigation across tools without bloating individual HTML pages.
- **App #1 (`strayd/studio.html`):** Builds wire-grade Press Release (top, dated) and platform-optimized social posts (below, undated) first, then uses those posts as spoken radio filler (`Speak`) second.

## 4. Press Release vs. Social Media Law
- **Press Release (Top, Dated):** Wire-grade release (`FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE`, full date, headline, Martin Strayd credentials, song spotlight, MP3 + cover art ownership/sharing rights for non-commercial friend enjoyment, and operator-selected links).
- **Social Media Posts (Below, Undated):** Never carry date stamps. Use each platform's character runway intentionally—short punchy hooks on X, richer storytelling, sales psychology, SEO keywords, dry humor, and listener value on long-form platforms—with optional toggles for Artist Hub, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal, and Wise.
