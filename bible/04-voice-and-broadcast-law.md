# Bible Law: Female Radio Host Voice & 365-Day Broadcast Rules

## 1. DJ Bananna Voice Law (All Apps Must Follow)
- **Female Voice Only:** DJ Bananna is strictly a female radio host. Never select a known male voice when any female or non-male voice is available.
- **Natural / Neural Preference:** Prefer natural, neural, online, enhanced, or female voices (such as Aria, Jenny, Sonia, Libby, Maisie, Natasha, Samantha, Victoria, Zira) over harsh or male voices.
- **Radio Filler Order:** Build social media posts first; use those posts as radio filler second via `Speak` (stripping URLs, hashtags, and raw punctuation symbols).

## 2. Press Release vs. Social Posts Law
- **Press Release First & Dated:** Place the Press Release above social posts and include the full release date only there.
- **Social Posts Undated:** Never stamp dates onto X, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Title, or YouTube Description posts. Lead with clean song titles, strong hooks/keywords, and DJ Bananna daily-launch marketing.
- **Optional Links Only When Selected:** Append Artist Hub, Buy Me a Coffee, PayPal, or Wise links only when explicitly toggled on by the operator.

## 3. 365-Day Catalog & Ownership Continuity
- **Single Reference Catalog:** Pull track metadata and cover art from `strayd/songs-db.json` and `strayd/core-catalog.json` with safe artwork fallbacks when a path is missing.
- **Visual Law:** Realistic, cinematic aesthetics only—zero cartoon imagery.
- **Listener Rights:** Listeners own MP3 + cover art bundles and may share freely with friends for non-commercial enjoyment.
