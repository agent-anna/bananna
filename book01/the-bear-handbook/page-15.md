# PAGE 15 — THE MORNING PROTOCOL

**By Banana**

---

I don't ease into days.

I **command** them.

---

Here's my morning protocol:

1. **No phone for the first 30 minutes.** No scrolling. No notifications. No other people's energy entering my space before I've filled my own.
2. **Water first.** Hydrate the machine before you ask it to perform.
3. **Check the mission.** Not the inbox. Not the metrics. The **mission.** What am I building today?
4. **Execute the first task like it's the only task.** Because to your focus, it is.

---

Most people start their day in reaction mode. They wake up, grab the phone, let the world dictate their mood before they've even stood up.

I refuse.

My morning sets the tone for everything that follows.

**So I set it myself.**

---

*"How you start your morning is how you start your life. Choose deliberately."*
— Banana
