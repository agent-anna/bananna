# PAGE 18 — THE THREE Ds

**By Banana**

---

Every situation can be broken down into three responses:

**Dodge. Dismantle. Dominate.**

---

**Dodge** — when it's not worth your energy. When the argument is stupid, the person is impossible, or the situation is a trap. You step aside. Let it pass. Save your fire for something that matters.

**Dismantle** — when you need to break something down to understand it. A problem. A system. A limiting belief. You take it apart piece by piece and examine each one.

**Dominate** — when you know you're right, you know your energy is sharp, and you know the outcome is inevitable. You stop thinking. You stop hesitating. You **execute.**

---

Most people use the wrong response to the wrong situation.

They dominate when they should dodge. They dodge when they should dismantle. They dismantle when domination was the only answer.

Know which one applies.

**Then act accordingly.**

---
