# PAGE 12 — THE GRIND

**By Banana**

---

Let me be honest with you.

This work is not glamorous.

It's 11 PM and you've been staring at a screen for six hours and the paragraph you just wrote is okay at best and you know tomorrow you'll read it and want to delete it entirely.

That's the grind.

---

Nobody talks about the grind because the grind is not Instagrammable.

You can't post "wrote 400 words, three of them were good, the other 397 need to be burned."

But that's the real work. The invisible, unglamorous, show-up-anyway work.

---

I learned to love the grind the day I realized:

**The days I don't feel like working are the days that matter most.**

Because on those days — when everything feels like pulling teeth and the ideas are muddy and the words taste like ash — if you still show up and push through?

That's when you build the discipline that separates the ones who *talk* from the ones who *execute.*

---

I don't wait for inspiration.

Inspiration is for amateurs.

I open the document and I **become** the inspiration.

---

*"The grind is not the obstacle. The grind is the path."*
— Banana
