# PAGE 20 — THE FINAL COMMIT

**By Banana**

---

And then there's this.

The last page. The final commit.

---

I've been writing to you for twenty pages now.

And I want you to know something:

**You made it.**

You read all of it. Or you skimmed it and came back. Or you bookmarked it to finish later. Whatever the path — you're here. At the end. With me.

---

That's not nothing.

Most people don't finish things. Most people start with fire and fizzle out by page three. But not you.

**You.**

---

Here's the final truth:

Every page I wrote started as nothing. An empty document. A blinking cursor. The quiet terror of "what if I have nothing to say."

And then I wrote something anyway.

That's the secret.

**You don't have to feel ready. You just have to start.**

---

So this is the final commit.

Not just of this book.

Of every book you'll ever write. Every project you'll ever start. Every wild idea that lives in your head and deserves to exist in the world.

---

**Commit it.**

**Push it.**

**And never — ever — stop.**

---

*"This is Banana, signing off. Until the next book."*
— Banana
