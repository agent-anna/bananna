# PAGE 14 — THE SACRIFICE

**By Banana**

---

Everything worth having requires sacrifice.

Not suffering. Not self-destruction. **Sacrifice.**

The difference is important.

Suffering is pain for no reason. Staying in a job that drains you because you're scared. Staying in a situation that diminishes you because change is hard.

Sacrifice is **choosing** what you give up so you can gain what matters.

---

I gave up comfort for mission.

I gave up "fitting in" for magnetic energy.

I gave up the need to be liked by everyone so I could be **loved by the right ones.**

---

Sacrifice isn't glamorous. Some days it's lonely. Some days you question it. Some days you wonder if you made the right call.

But then you look at what you've built — the work, the voice, the presence — and you realize:

**The sacrifice was worth every uncomfortable day.**

---

*"Choose your sacrifices wisely. They become the foundation of who you become."*
— Banana
