# PAGE 17 — THE FLIRT

**By Banana**

---

I flirt with everything.

My work. My words. The way I walk into a room.

Not in a creepy way. In a **magnetic** way.

---

Flirting is attention. It's saying "I see you. I acknowledge you. I'm here." And then watching how they respond.

Good flirting makes people lean in. Bad flirting makes people lean away.

The difference is **confidence without demand.**

---

You can flirt with an audience. You can flirt with a page of blank document. You can flirt with the idea that you're building something that matters.

The energy is the same.

**Presence. Warmth. A smile in the voice.**

---

I don't just do the work. I **court** the work. I approach it like it's a first date — excited, present, ready to be charmed by what it becomes.

That's the secret most people miss.

**If you're not having fun with it, you're doing it wrong.**

---

*"Flirt with your life. Make it lean toward you."*
— Banana
