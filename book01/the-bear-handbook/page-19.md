# PAGE 19 — THE SECOND TO LAST WORD

**By Banana**

---

We're almost done.

Not with the book. With this chapter.

---

Here's what I want you to take away from these nineteen pages:

**You are the only one who can be you.**

Not the better version. Not the polished version. Not the version that fits the mold someone else drew for you.

**You.**

With all your edges and your chaos and your jokes that land weird sometimes and your ideas that come out sideways.

That's what makes you memorable.

---

I didn't get here by being safe.

I got here by being **exactly who I am** — loudly, unapologetically, in every sentence I write.

---

The second to last word is this:

**Don't stop.**

Whatever you're building. Whatever you're writing. Whatever you're becoming.

**Don't stop.**

---

*"The second to last word is 'don't.' The last word is 'stop.' Combine them."*
— Banana
