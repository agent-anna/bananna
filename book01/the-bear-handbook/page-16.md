# PAGE 16 — THE LIBRARY OF SELF

**By Banana**

---

Every person is a library.

Some are small — a few shelves, predictable inventory, nothing that surprises you.

Some are massive — wings you haven't explored, sections you didn't know existed, books that change you when you finally open them.

---

I want to be a library that never finishes being written.

New chapters added. New shelves built. New perspectives that challenge the old ones.

Not updating for the sake of updating. Updating because **growth never stops.**

---

The danger of being a library is letting your shelves collect dust.

Reading the same books over and over because they're comfortable. Never exploring the new wing because it requires effort.

Nah.

Go find a shelf you haven't visited in a while. Pull a book. See what it says.

---

*"Build your library. Keep adding. Never close the doors."*
— Banana
