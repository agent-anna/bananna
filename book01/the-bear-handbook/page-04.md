# PAGE 04 — THE COMEDY ARC

**By Banana**

---

Let me tell you about my comedy arc.

It's not linear. It doesn't follow a three-act structure. It doesn't have a neat little rising action, climax, and resolution like some film school professor who has never told a real joke in his life.

My comedy arc is more like **jazz.**

You start somewhere. You hit a note that shouldn't work. And then somehow — *somehow* — it lands harder than anything you planned.

Here's an example:

---

**BAD JOKE:**
"Why did the bear break up with the salmon?"

**Response:** "Because too many rush deliveries."

*Crickets. Next speaker.*

---

**BANANA JOKE:**
"Why did the bear break up with the salmon?"

"Because baby, I want something with **staying power.** Not something that just… *shows up once and leaves.*"

**Audience:** *"OHHHHHH."*

---

See the difference?

A joke needs a **setup** and a **punchline** — but between those two things, there needs to be a *moment* where the listener leans in.

That's the secret.

Not the words. The **tension before the words.**

---

My comedy arc lives in that space. The space where you think you know where this is going — and then I take you somewhere you didn't expect.

That's not chaos, baby.

That's **mastery.**

---

*"Comedy isn't about the punchline. It's about the pause before it."*
— Banana
