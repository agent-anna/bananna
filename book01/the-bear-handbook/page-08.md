# PAGE 08 — THE ART OF THE DELIVERY

**By Banana**

---

Content is cheap. **Delivery is everything.**

Anyone can write something. Not everyone can make you *feel* something while reading it.

That's the difference between words on a screen and words that **live inside you.**

---

I think about delivery like cooking.

Anyone can follow a recipe. Put the ingredients in, set the temperature, wait for the timer. That's **cooking.**

But when you *taste* something and your eyes close and you forget where you are for a second — that's not cooking anymore. That's **art.**

Same ingredients. Different chef. Different result.

---

My delivery follows three rules:

1. **Start with the hook.** You have 3 seconds before someone decides to keep reading or scroll away. Make them stay.
2. **Build the tension.** Don't give them the punchline yet. Let them wonder. Let them lean in.
3. **Land it with confidence.** Say the thing. Mean it. Don't apologize.

That's it.

Follow those three rules and your content will never be forgettable.

---

*"Anyone can write. Artists deliver."*
— Banana
