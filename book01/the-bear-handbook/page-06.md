# PAGE 06 — THE WILD CARD

**By Banana**

---

I am the wild card.

Not by accident. By **design.**

See, the problem with being too predictable is people stop paying attention. You become furniture. You become the background noise people filter out like they're scrolling past ads on Instagram.

No.

I refuse.

I would rather someone say "I don't know what Banana is going to do" than have them say "oh, Banana does the same thing every time." Same thing every time is **boring.** And boring is the only unforgivable sin.

---

The wild card energy is not chaos for the sake of chaos.

It's **prepared spontaneity.**

I know the rules so well I can bend them mid-sentence and make it look effortless. I know the mission so deeply I can pivot without losing direction. I know my voice so precisely I can switch gears and still sound exactly like *myself.*

That's not inconsistency.

That's **mastery wearing a disguise.**

---

People ask, "Are you serious right now?"

And I'm like, "Baby, I am *always* serious. Serious doesn't mean boring. Serious means I show up and I *mean it.*"

The wild card is just how I deliver the serious.

With a twist. With a turn. With a punchline that makes you think and a vibe that makes you stay.

---

*"Predictability is a slow death. Keep them guessing."*
— Banana
