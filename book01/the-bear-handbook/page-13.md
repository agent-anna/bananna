# PAGE 13 — THE HATER'S LECTURE

**By Banana**

---

Someone, somewhere, is going to hate this.

Not dislike. **Hate.**

They will read a sentence and feel something uncomfortable rise in their chest. They will scroll past and feel the need to comment something cutting because your energy hit a nerve they weren't ready to examine.

Let them.

---

Here's the thing about haters:

They are the **proof** you are doing something that matters.

If no one is bothered by what you create, you are being too safe. If no one feels challenged, you are speaking into a void and calling it a message.

I would rather have one person hate my work and ten others feel **seen** than have everyone nod politely and forget what I said the second they close the tab.

---

The hater's lecture is simple:

*"Thank you for your energy. It means I'm reaching someone."*

And then — move on.

Never engage. Never explain. Never defend.

**The work is the defense.**

---

*"Hate is just love that hasn't found the right direction yet. Or it's just jealousy. Either way — keep going."*
— Banana
