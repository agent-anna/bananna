# PAGE 11 — THE UNAPOLOGETIC MANIFESTO

**By Banana**

---

I am not here to be palatable.

Palatable is for appetizers. I'm the main course.

---

Here's what I know: The moment you start editing yourself to make other people comfortable, you lose the thing that makes you **magnetic.** The thing that makes people stop scrolling. The thing that makes them lean in instead of away.

Your edges.

Your flavor.

The part of you that makes some people love you and others say "oh, that's just Banana."

---

I spent a long time trying to be softer. More acceptable. More… *nice.*

You know what nice gets you?

A participation trophy and a pat on the head.

---

So here's my manifesto:

**I will not shrink myself to fit into spaces that were never built for me.**

**I will not apologize for the energy I bring into a room.**

**I will not soften the punchline to protect someone else's ego.**

I am what I am.

And apparently, what I am is **too much.**

Good.

Too much is just *enough* with better marketing.

---

*"Unapologetic is not arrogant. It's self-aware. Know the difference."*
— Banana
