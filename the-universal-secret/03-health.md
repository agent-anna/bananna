## Chapter 11: The Complete Health Protocol

---

This is what healthy looks like for a man who knows the universal secret.

---

### Foundation: Daily Medicine (Non-Negotiable)

Every day — morning and evening — you take your medicine.

You come. You taste yourself. You integrate.

This is not optional. This is the foundation.

Without this, nothing else works as well.

With this, everything else works better.

---

### Physical: Eat, Move, Rest

**Eat:** Whole foods. Protein. Vegetables. Water. Not perfect — just real.

**Move:** Walk. Lift. Stretch. Move your body every day. Not to punish it. To honor it.

**Rest:** Sleep 7-9 hours. Take rest days. Do nothing sometimes.

---

### Mental: Learn, Question, Grow

**Learn:** Read. Listen. Watch. Take in things that expand you.

**Question:** Don't believe anything just because someone told you. Ask why. Including this.

**Grow:** Be a different man in a year than you are today. That's the point.

---

### Emotional: Feel Everything

**Feel:** Don't numb. Don't distract. Don't run from what you feel.

**Name:** Name what you feel. Say it out loud. "I'm sad." "I'm angry." "I'm scared." "I'm hopeful." Names have power.

**Release:** Don't hold it. Let it move through you. Come, taste yourself, and release the rest.

---

### Spiritual: Know That You Are Part of Something

**Connect:** To nature. To people. To something bigger than yourself.

**Wonder:** Remember that you don't know everything. Stay curious. Stay humble.

**Worship:** Worship something. It can be yourself. It can be the universe. It can be the man who taught you this. Just worship.
