# PART TWO: THE DAILY RITUALS
## How to Take Your Medicine Every Day

---

## Chapter 6: Why Daily?

---

Health is not a one-time event.

Health is a practice.

You don't exercise once and call yourself fit. You don't eat one salad and call yourself healthy. You don't meditate once and call yourself calm.

It's the same with this.

One time tasting yourself is powerful. But daily practice is *transformative.*

Here's why:

---

**Repetition rewires the nervous system.**

Every time you take your medicine — every time you come and then taste yourself and say *yes, this is mine* — you're rewiring a circuit that's been broken since you were a teenager.

The first time, your body is skeptical. It's been hearing "shame, shame, shame" for decades. It doesn't believe you yet.

The fifth time, it starts to wonder.

The twentieth time, it starts to accept.

The fiftieth time, it stops asking.

By then, the shame is gone. And your body gives you the full dose every time.

---

**Ritual creates identity.**

If you do this once, you're a man who tasted himself once.

If you do it daily, you're a man who *knows himself.*

That's not a small difference.

That changes everything: how you carry your body, how you speak, how you move through the world, how you love, how you connect.

Daily practice creates the man you're becoming.

---

## Chapter 7: The Morning Ritual

---

**When:** First thing upon waking
**Duration:** 10 minutes
**Purpose:** Set the tone. Take your first dose. Start the day grounded, calm, whole.

---

### Step 1: Wake in Your Body (2 minutes)

Lie in bed before the phone. Before the day.

Put one hand on your chest. One hand on your stomach.

Breathe.

Feel your heartbeat. Feel your lungs. Feel your body being alive.

Say out loud:

**"I am alive. My body is not shameful. I take my medicine without shame."**

Feel the words in your mouth. Feel them land in your chest.

---

### Step 2: The Morning Touch (5 minutes)

Touch yourself slowly. Feel your skin wake up. Feel the heat in your hands.

No goal. No rushing.

Just arrival. Just presence. Just you, finally visiting yourself.

Feel your chest. Your stomach. Your thighs. The parts of you that the world doesn't let you touch.

Touch them now.

Be gentle. Be thorough.

Be home.

---

### Step 3: Come and Take Your Medicine (3 minutes)

When you feel ready — when your body says yes — let yourself come.

Slowly.

Take your time.

This is not a task. This is worship.

And when you're done — when the medicine is on your skin, in your hands, proof that you are alive and capable of feeling everything — don't wipe it away.

**Put your fingers to your lips.**

Taste yourself.

One finger. Then another. Then another.

Let the medicine enter your body fully.

Let it teach you who you are.

---

### The Morning Promise:

Do this every day for thirty days, and notice:

- Lower morning anxiety
- More presence throughout the day
- Better mood
- More self-acceptance
- Stronger sense of self
- Less need for external validation
- More groundedness

This is not magic. This is medicine.

You just weren't taking it.

Now you are.

## Chapter 8: The Evening Ritual

---

**When:** Before bed
**Duration:** 15 minutes
**Purpose:** Unwind. Release the day. Take your medicine. Sleep healed.

---

### Step 1: The Doorway Transition (1 minute)

Walk through your door. Stop at the threshold.

Place your hand on the frame.

Say:

**"I'm home."**

Not to the house. To yourself.

You've been gone all day. Wearing masks. Playing roles. Being who everyone else needed.

Now you come home.

Now you're just you.

---

### Step 2: The Unclothing (3 minutes)

Take off your clothes slowly.

Feel each layer fall away.

Feel the air on your skin.

This is the armor coming off. This is the performance ending.

You're not the person you were today.

You're just the man underneath.

---

### Step 3: The Evening Touch (8 minutes)

Lie down. Close your eyes. Put on music if you want — or don't.

This is not rushed.

This is a visit.

Touch your whole body slowly. Feel where you hold tension. Feel where you let go.

This is not about coming fast. This is about *visiting* yourself. Being with yourself. Letting yourself feel everything you've been too busy to feel.

Take your time.

The medicine will come when it's ready.

Let it.

---

### Step 4: Taste and Rest (3 minutes)

When you come — when the medicine arrives — don't look away.

Look at it.

That's proof you're alive. That's your body's gift. That's the healing you're supposed to take.

Now.

Open your mouth.

Put your fingers to your lips.

Taste yourself.

Slowly. Thoroughly. All of it.

Let it enter you fully.

And then — only then — close your eyes and drift into sleep knowing:

*I am not ashamed. I took my medicine today. I am healing.*

---

## Chapter 9: The Weekly Deep Dive

---

**When:** Sunday night
**Duration:** 30 minutes
**Purpose:** Integrate the week. Release what's done. Go deeper.

---

Run a warm bath. Light a candle. Play music that makes you feel something.

Lower yourself in.

Feel the water hold you.

Touch yourself slowly — in the water, in the warmth, in the safety of your own skin.

Let yourself come.

And as you come — watch.

Watch your healing fluid dissolve in the water.

Watch yourself releasing.

Watch yourself letting go.

Say:

**"I release everything this week. I am clean. I am whole. I am ready for the next."**

This is the ritual of completion.

This is how you let go of what's done.

This is healing.

Do it weekly.
