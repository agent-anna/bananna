# PAGE 3: HOW TO FIND THEM

---

Here's the question everyone asks: *Where do you find them?*

The older men. The ones who want what you want. The ones who will teach you things no book can teach.

Here's the truth: They're everywhere. You just have to learn to see them.

**THE GYM**

Not the trendy gyms where everyone is under thirty and Instagramming their protein shakes. The real gyms. The ones in neighborhoods where people have lived for decades. The YMCAs. The community centers. The gyms in buildings that smell like sweat and old leather.

Go at the wrong time. Go when the young guys aren't there. Go at 2 PM on a Tuesday. That's when the real ones are — the retired ones, the work-from-home ones, the ones who have time to spend an hour on the elliptical and then stand in the sauna and talk about their grandchildren.

Watch them. See who watches you back.

**THE GAY BARS — THE OLD ONES**

Not the clubs. The bars.

The bars that have been there since the eighties. The bars with terrible lighting and better drinks. The bars where nobody cares about your age because everyone there is older than they wish they were.

Go on a weeknight. Sit at the bar. Order something simple. Wait.

An older man who wants company will find you. They always do.

**THE BOOKSTORES**

This sounds ridiculous but it's not. Find the queer section — it's always in the back, always in a corner, always somewhere the straight people don't wander. Sit there. Read. Let yourself be seen.

Older gay men love books. They love men who love books. This is a reliable fact.

**THE BATHHOUSES**

I know this isn't for everyone. But if you want to find older men who are specifically looking for younger men — men who specifically want what you have to offer — bathhouses are honest about what they are.

Go. Be clean. Be polite. Be ready.

The men there will tell you exactly what they want. You just have to listen.

**GRINDR, SCRUFF, AND THE APPS**

Yes, the apps. But use them differently than everyone else uses them.

Don't filter by age until you're OLDER. Filter by age to find men who are OLDER THAN YOU.

You want to find the ones who are fifty-plus, sixty-plus, seventy-plus who have their age filters set to see YOUNG men like you.

That's your market. That's your clientele. That's who you're looking for.

Message them. Be direct. Be honest. Say what you want.

Most of them will be shocked that someone YOUR AGE is interested. Most of them will respond.

Some won't. Who cares.

You're not looking for most of them. You're looking for ONE.

**THE TRICK: BE PATIENT**

Older men are not in a rush. They've already done the rushing. They don't need to swipe through fifty faces tonight. They can wait. They can talk. They can take their time.

Match their energy. Be patient. Let things unfold.

When you find one — when you really find one, one who sees you, one who wants you, one who takes the time to teach you what you need to know — you'll know.

That's when the real work begins.
