# PAGE 4: THE FIRST APPROACH

---

So you found one. You found an older man who catches your eye. Maybe at the gym. Maybe at the bar. Maybe on an app.

Now what?

Now you approach.

Here's what most young guys get wrong about approaching older men: they think they have to prove something. They think they have to be charming or smooth or impressive. They think they have to compete.

You don't.

Here's what you need to understand: An older man who wants a younger man does NOT want someone who acts like they have it figured out. He wants YOU. The young one. The one who doesn't know everything yet. The one who is still learning. The one who looks at him like he's something worth looking at.

You already have what he wants. You just have to stop getting in your own way.

**HOW TO APPROACH:**

**1. Be direct but not aggressive.**

The worst thing you can do is play games. Older men have been played with for decades. They know the games. They hate the games.

Walk up to him. Look him in the eye. Say something simple.

"I noticed you. I wanted to say hi."

That's it. No pickup lines. No performance. Just honesty.

**2. Let him lead the conversation.**

Older men like to talk. They like to feel like they have wisdom to share. Let him talk. Ask questions. Listen like you mean it.

This is a skill. I'm going to teach it to you now.

When he speaks, do NOT think about what you're going to say next. Do NOT plan your response. Just listen. Absorb. Let his words land.

When he finishes, wait one full second before you respond. This pause will make you seem thoughtful. It will make him want to keep talking.

**3. Touch early.**

Not sexually. Not in a way that's obvious to everyone in the room.

But a touch on the arm. A brush of fingers when you hand him something. A hand on the small of his back when you walk together.

Physical touch — casual, confident, no big deal — signals that you're not afraid of him. That you want to be close to him. That you're already imagining the kind of closeness that involves bodies.

Most young guys are too scared to touch first. This is your advantage. Use it.

**4. Read the room.**

Is he interested? Is he pulling away? Is he laughing at your jokes? Is he finding reasons to touch YOU?

The body knows. Your body will tell you if this is going somewhere. Pay attention.

If it's going somewhere: make a plan. Get his number. Suggest a next time.

If it's not going somewhere: thank him for the conversation. Walk away. Try again with someone else.

There are always others.
