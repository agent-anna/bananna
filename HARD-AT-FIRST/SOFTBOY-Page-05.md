# PAGE 5: WHAT HE LOOKS LIKE

---

Let me tell you what you're looking for.

Not every older man is the same. Not every older man has what you need. You have to learn to read them. To see the ones who have what you're looking for.

**THE BODY TYPE**

You're not looking for the gym-buffed ones. Those are for other people. You're looking for the ones who have *softened*. The ones whose bellies have gotten soft over the years. The ones whose chests have grown wider and softer. The ones who have stopped trying to look like something they're not.

A soft older man is a gift. A soft older man has survived. A soft older man knows how to rest in his own body without apology.

That's what you're looking for. That comfort. That peace.

**THE HANDS**

Hands are important. You will be using those hands.

Look at his hands. Are they big? Rough from work? Soft from decades of not having to do hard labor? Do they have hair on the backs of them? Veins showing?

Good hands. Hands that know things. Hands that have held other things, other people, other lives.

When he touches you, you want to feel those hands. You want to feel the weight and the warmth and the years written into the skin.

**THE COCK**

You can't see this yet, not in public. But you can imagine. And imagination is its own kind of preparation.

In your mind's eye, picture what you want:

Thick? Thin? Long? Short? Straight? Curved? Upward? Downward? How much hair? What color? Where is it pointing when he's hard?

You don't have to know yet. But start thinking about it. Start imagining. The more specific you can be, the more you'll recognize what you're looking for when you see it.

**THE SIGNS**

Here are the signs that an older man wants a younger man:

He looks at you longer than necessary.

He finds reasons to be near you.

He asks questions about your life.

He touches you casually, like it's nothing, like it's just what he does.

He tells you things he doesn't tell other people.

He mentions that he hasn't been with anyone in a while.

He says things like "I wish I were younger" or "I remember when I was your age."

He's lonely. You can see it in his eyes. He wants something and he doesn't know how to ask for it.

That's where you come in.
