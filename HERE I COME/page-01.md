I just knew, in that moment, watching his hands on her hip, that there was something inside me that was waking up. Something hungry. Something that knew, even at sixteen, that it was going to get what it wanted.

Eventually.

Somehow.

I am fifty-two years old now and I am finally writing it down.

And now it is your turn.
